## ⚡ Design Archetypes: Dual Identity Engine

- **Cyber-Dossier 2026 (Default Mode)**:
  - Inspired by the Meta AI / Muse shared creation (`muse.ai/s/portfolio-xhxa6d0xux0xdxzhj`).
  - Dark Void Terminal (`#040608`), neon biometric green (`#00FF88`), radar grid overlays.
  - Interactive SVG dot-matrix biometric avatar matching the Muse artifact abstraction.
  - Live identity scanning bar: `IDENTITY SCAN / DOSSIER 2026`.
- **Grunge Zine Mode**:
  - Raw, chaotic energy & stop-motion physical media.
  - Aged off-white paper (`#F0EAD6`), charcoal black (`#1A1A1A`), crimson tape stamps.
  - Instant one-click toggle in the masthead navigation and mobile drawer.

- **Core Concept**: Raw, Chaotic Energy & Stop-Motion Physical Media.
- **Palette**:
  - Off-White Paper: `#F0EAD6` (Warm aged paper texture with grit & fibers)
  - Charcoal Black: `#1A1A1A` (Deep distressed black for text & graphic elements)
  - Crimson Red: `#DC143C` (Textured ink-bleed banners, highlights, and rubber stamps)
  - Muted Gold: `#C5A945` (Vintage annotations, stamps, and highlights)
- **Typography**:
  - Headlines: **'Oswald'** / **'Impact'** (Massive, distressed block letters filling viewport width)
  - Script Elements: **'Permanent Marker'** / **'Rock Salt'** (Raw brush script elements)
  - Technical Data: **'Roboto Mono'** (Small mono-spaced labels, technical annotations, and dates)
- **Textures & Elements**:
  - Global grit, dust, and halftone dot pattern overlays.
  - Torn paper edge clip-paths and tape stamps.
  - Rubber stamps ("AIR-GAPPED APPROVED", "FOOD KINETICS SMMI", "EXP. 2026").
  - Rock-solid stability: zero automatic involuntary movement or motion sickness triggers; tactile interaction happens only on user intent (hover/click).
  - Card hover state with crisp mechanical offsets and shadow shifts.
